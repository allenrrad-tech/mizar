Mizar Guide

Learn what every tool and screen does

Main Screens

Converter

The main screen. Type a value, pick your From and To units, then tap Convert — or enable auto-convert in Settings to calculate as you type.

History

Your last 50 conversions. Tap any entry to jump back to it. Use Clear all to wipe the log.

Collections

Ready-made sets grouped by topic: Cooking, Travel, Science, Everyday. Tap a pair to open it in the Converter.

Settings

Toggle Dark/Light theme, set Decimal Places, show/hide Convert button, enable Auto-Copy.

Converter Tools

Category Bar

Swipe the card bar at the top to browse 10 categories. Tap a card to switch.

Unit Picker

Tap the unit button (e.g. km) to open a searchable list. Type to filter, tap to select.

Swap Button

Swaps From and To units and moves the result into the input field.

Star — Favorites

Saves the unit pair as a favorite chip above the converter. Tap again to remove.

Copy Result

Copies the result to clipboard. Enable Auto-Copy in Settings for automatic copying.

Base Ratio Card

Shows the exact factor — e.g. 1 km = 1000 m. Updates live.
